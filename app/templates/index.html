About LawPilot AI

Intelligent, Multilingual Legal Assistant for Everyday Legal Rights in India.

Product Mission & Identity

LawPilot AI is designed to demystify Indian law for ordinary citizens. Millions of Indians face everyday disputes—landlords withholding security deposits, online retailers refusing refunds for defective goods, or employers delaying full and final settlements. Most people do not know their basic rights under the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, or the Payment of Wages Act, 1936.

LawPilot AI provides a clear bridge: simple natural language input in English, Hindi, or Hinglish, grounded statutory rights, evidence checklists, and professionally drafted notices.

Anti-Hallucination & Retrieval System

Standard LLMs frequently hallucinate legal sections or cite outdated statutes. LawPilot AI solves this through a dedicated statutory grounding engine:

  • Statutory Registry: Every cited section is retrieved from verified repositories on India Code (https://www.indiacode.nic.in/).
  • Refusal to Fabricate: The system refuses requests to invent laws or guarantee court victories.
  • Citation Verification: If a user cites a false section (e.g. "Section 999"), the anti-hallucination engine refutes it and cites the genuine provision.
  • State Specificity: Distinguishes Central Acts from State-specific tenancy acts (e.g. Maharashtra Rent Control Act, Delhi Rent Control Act).

Ethical Guardrails & Advocate Escalation

LawPilot AI does not replace licensed advocates. Whenever critical risks are detected—such as criminal summons, approaching court deadlines, threats of physical violence, or illegal lockouts—the system activates an emergency escalation alert directing the user to official helplines (NALSA 15100, Police 112) and licensed advocates.
