# LawPilot AI — 3-Minute Live Interview Walkthrough Script

**Presenter:** Full-Stack & AI Engineer  
**Target Audience:** Interview Panel / Product Evaluators  
**Scenario:** Landlord arbitrarily withholding ₹25,000 security deposit for normal painting wear-and-tear  
**Total Target Time:** 3 minutes (180 seconds)  

---

### [00:00 – 00:30] Introduction & Problem Setup

*(Start on the Homepage `http://localhost:8000`)*

> **"Hello everyone. Today I'm excited to present LawPilot AI — an intelligent, multilingual legal assistant built for everyday legal challenges in India.**
>
> **Every year, millions of Indians face disputes with landlords, online sellers, or employers. Most people don't know their statutory rights, cannot afford an advocate for a ₹25,000 dispute, and generic AI chatbots often hallucinate fake laws.**
>
> **Let's see how LawPilot AI solves this in real time."**

---

### [00:30 – 01:10] Describing the Problem in Hinglish & AI Grounding

*(Click 'Legal Assistant' tab or type into the main input)*

> **"A young professional moved out of a flat in Bengaluru, but their landlord refuses to return their deposit, claiming ₹15,000 was deducted for painting. The user speaks naturally in Hinglish:"**

*(Type or voice input):*
`"Mera landlord 25000 ka security deposit wapas nahi de raha hai. Bol raha hai 15000 painting ka katega, jabki flat ekdum clean return kiya tha. Koi written contract nahi tha, bas UPI transfer aur WhatsApp chat hai."`

*(Press Send. Point to the generated response)*

> **"Notice what happened instantly:**
> 1. **Statutory Anti-Hallucination Grounding:** LawPilot AI does *not* invent laws. It cites **Section 108(m) of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882**, explaining that routine painting is legally classified as *reasonable wear-and-tear*, which the landlord cannot unilaterally deduct.
> 2. **Official Citations:** Clickable badges link directly to the official government repository on **India Code**.
> 3. **Non-Repetitive Follow-ups:** The assistant identifies that the user hasn't specified their move-out handover proof, asking a concise follow-up in Hinglish."

---

### [01:10 – 01:45] Case Dashboard & Evidence Checklist

*(Click 'My Cases' tab)*

> - Category: Tenant Dispute
> - Parties: Tenant vs Landlord
